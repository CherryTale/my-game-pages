Project modifications for source packs 1–3: trimming, mono conversion, DC removal, band-pass filtering,
resampling, attack/release fades, layering and peak limiting. Output: 32 kHz PCM16 WAV.
Each output's exact source layers, timing and gains are in scripts/prepare-sound-effects.py.
Source/output hashes and processing parameters are kept in the source repository at public/assets/audio/sfx/processing.json.
These are edited material/foley sounds, not recordings of the game's specific trains.
Edits of source packs 1–3 are also provided under CC0 1.0.
The JC Sounds edits (item 4) remain CC BY 4.0.

5. Air (or steam) pressure release — brunoboselli (CC0 1.0)
   https://freesound.org/people/brunoboselli/sounds/457294/
   Source publication: January 25, 2019. License: Creative Commons 0.
   Studio recording of an inflatable stand-up board being deflated, edited for air/steam foley.
   Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/457/457294_300738-hq.mp3
   Save as steam-search/pressure-release.mp3 under the preparation script input directory.
   Output: steam-stop-soft.wav, also provided under CC0 1.0.
   Changes: extract 0.8–1.12 s, mono, DC removal, 180 Hz highpass / 1800 Hz lowpass,
   38 ms attack, power 1.6 decay, 50 ms release, level adjustment, 32 kHz PCM16.
   Used for the soft pressure release when reaching a facility stop.

6. Train whistle, foley #6 — Joseph SARDIN & Axeline T. (CC0)
   https://bigsoundbank.com/train-whistle-foley-6-s3320.html
   Download: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/bwf-en/3320.wav
   Save as whistle-v4/foley.wav under the preparation script input directory.
   Output: train-whistle-b-short.wav, also provided under CC0.
   Changes: source 0.72–0.97 s + 1.83–2.08 s, 30 ms crossfade to shorten sustain;
   mono, DC removal, 100 Hz highpass / 2600 Hz lowpass, 30 ms attack / 90 ms release,
   RMS 0.04 / peak cap 0.35, 32 kHz PCM16, 6 ms end silence. Original pitch retained.
   Duration: 0.476 s. Used when a train starts or resumes actual motion, and when dispatched.
